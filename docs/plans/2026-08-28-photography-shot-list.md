# Photography shot list

The restructure (docs/plans/2026-08-28-proposition-and-site-restructure.md)
identified the photography deficit as the single biggest polish gap between
this site and the premium spirits sites it is measured against. This brief is
the fix: twelve shots, each with a named slot on the site, plus the props to
buy and the rules that keep every frame claim-safe.

Nothing here needs a studio. Window light, a tripod, and patience produce
every shot on this list. A phone in RAW mode is acceptable for everything
except the two hero shots, where a borrowed or hired camera earns its keep.

## The rules before the shots

1. **Photograph only what is true.** No ingredient that is not in the bottle
   (the seven spices are vanilla pods, cinnamon, root ginger, orange peel,
   cloves, allspice, cassia bark; no star anise, no coffee beans, no
   nutmeg for styling because they look nice). No still, no barrels, no
   distillery imagery: production claims have their own rules and a
   photograph asserts them as strongly as a sentence.
2. **Use what we sell as props.** The Crystal ICE hiball, the Club ICE
   tumbler, the slate coasters, the jigger and bar blade are all product.
   Every serve shot styled with them sells twice.
3. **One light direction.** Window light from one side, a white board
   opposite to lift shadows, a black board to deepen them. Never mix
   daylight with a warm room bulb.
4. **Shoot every setup three ways**: wide/landscape for section backgrounds,
   4:5 portrait for product blocks and social, square for cards. Recompose,
   do not crop later.
5. **Keep the label straight and the bottle wiped.** Fingerprints on glass
   are the difference between premium and homemade at macro distance.

## The shots

Ordered by impact. The first four justify the whole exercise.

### 1. The hero serve
Storm & Spice built in our own Crystal ICE hiball: double measure, ginger
beer, fresh lime, cubed ice. Bottle standing behind, label soft but legible.
Dark surface, dark background, side light catching the condensation.
**Slot:** homepage hero background, product page serves section.
**Format:** landscape wide plus 4:5.

### 2. The bottle in scene
The bottle on dark wood beside a genuine jerry can, folded map or waxed
canvas underneath. Label face on, wax seal visible. This replaces the
white-background carousel render as the site's signature product image.
**Slot:** homepage product block, Open Graph image, press kit.
**Format:** 4:5 and square.

### 3. Wax seal and cork, macro
The detail reviewers name unprompted ("the chunky bottle, the waxed cork and
the lovely labels"). Fill the frame with the seal, drips and all; second
frame of the cork half pulled.
**Slot:** product page making section, gifts page.
**Format:** square and 4:5.

### 4. The batch number, macro
Tight on the label's numbering: batch and bottle number sharp, everything
else falling away. This is the scarcity claim, photographed instead of
asserted, and it feeds the Nomadic-style numbered block on the product page.
**Slot:** product page batch section, Expedition Log.
**Format:** square.

### 5. The neat serve
Rocks glass (Club ICE tumbler), one large clear ice cube, orange peel
expressed and dropped in, exactly as the house serve describes. Bottle soft
in the background.
**Slot:** serves section, tasting notes, cocktail pages.
**Format:** 4:5.

### 6. The medal serve
The rum and cola exactly as judged: 50ml rum, 150ml cola, no ice, with the
Franklin and Sons bottle beside the glass. This is the photograph of the
IWSC Silver, so precision matters more than styling.
**Slot:** medal bar context, trade page, the serve's own content.
**Format:** landscape and 4:5.

### 7. The pour
Action frame: rum arcing from bottle to glass, light behind the stream.
Burst mode, many takes, one keeper.
**Slot:** hero alternate, social, section dividers.
**Format:** 4:5 and landscape.

### 8. The gift
Presentation box open with the bottle seated in it; second setup with the
gift pack barware arranged around it. Styled for the wedding-gift buyer the
testimonial speaks to: considered, not ribboned to death.
**Slot:** gifts page, gift product pages, seasonal campaigns.
**Format:** landscape and square.

### 9. The seven spices, flat lay
The bottle centred, the actual seven arranged around it: vanilla pods,
cinnamon or cassia quills, root ginger, dried orange peel, cloves, allspice
berries. Shot from directly above on slate or dark wood. This is the
ingredients claim as a picture, and it must match the ingredients page
exactly.
**Slot:** story section, ingredients content, press kit.
**Format:** square and landscape.

### 10. The founders
Dan and Rhys, outdoors or against something honest (workshop, field, brick),
natural light, not posed to death. One frame together, one each. The
restructure's story section and the Nomadic-style collaborator block need
faces; the press kit needs them more.
**Slot:** story section, About, press kit, PR outreach.
**Format:** landscape and 4:5.

### 11. In the field
The bottle with expedition kit: enamel mugs, pack, boots, weather. The brand
texture shot, honest to the name. Best shot on an actual walk rather than
staged in a garden.
**Slot:** section backgrounds, social, email headers.
**Format:** landscape wide.

### 12. Glassware at work
Each glassware product holding a real serve, same light and surface as the
serve shots so the shop grid photographs as a family.
**Slot:** shop collection cards, product pages.
**Format:** square.

## The shopping list

Likely already owned: a genuine jerry can, the glassware, coasters, jigger,
bar blade, maps and kit for shots 2 and 11.

To buy:

- **Large clear-ice cube mould** (two inch). Cloudy ice reads cheap at macro
  distance; a directional-freezing mould is under £15.
- **Fresh produce on the day:** oranges, limes, root ginger.
- **Whole spices matching the seven:** vanilla pods, cinnamon or cassia
  quills, cloves, allspice berries, dried orange peel. Supermarket spice
  aisle covers all of it.
- **Franklin and Sons cola** and a premium ginger beer for the serve shots.
- **A dark wood surface:** one stained scaffold board or a large dark
  chopping board.
- **A dark cloth:** waxed cotton or dark linen, one metre is plenty.
- **One white and one black A2 foam board** for shaping the window light.
- **A phone tripod** with a remote or timer, if none owned.

Total well under £100 excluding the tripod.

## Not on this list, deliberately

- Studio lighting, backdrops, or hired kit: window light is the look.
- Model or lifestyle shoots with people other than the founders: later, if
  ever, and never before the core twelve exist.
- Any prop implying provenance: barrels, stills, botanical fields, harbour
  scenes. The photograph makes the claim; the claim has rules.
